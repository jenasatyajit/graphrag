# Expense Approval SOP

Document type: Standard Operating Procedure
SOP version: v2.1
Effective date: 2024-03-01
Process: Expense Approval

## Overview

This SOP defines the Expense Approval process for reimbursing employee business
expenses. The Expense Approval process must satisfy the SOX Financial Controls
compliance requirement (regulation reference SOX-404).

## Step 1: Submit Expense Report

The employee submits an expense report in the Concur system. Each line item must
include a receipt image and a business justification. This step is owned by the
Employee role and requires the Concur system.

## Step 2: Manager Review

The direct manager reviews the submitted expense report for policy compliance.
Expenses above $500 require additional documentation. This step is owned by the
Manager role and requires the Concur system.

## Step 3: Finance Approval

The finance team performs the final approval and schedules reimbursement. This
step is owned by the Finance Analyst role and requires the SAP system. Approved
reimbursements are paid within 10 business days.

## Step 4: Payment Reconciliation

Finance reconciles the reimbursement payment against the SAP ledger. This step is
owned by the Finance Analyst role and requires the SAP system. The Expense
Approval process must satisfy the SOX Financial Controls compliance requirement.
