# Expense Approval SOP

Document type: Standard Operating Procedure
SOP version: v3.0
Effective date: 2025-06-01
Process: Expense Approval

## Overview

This SOP defines the current Expense Approval process for reimbursing employee
business expenses. It supersedes version v2.1. The Expense Approval process must
satisfy the SOX Financial Controls compliance requirement (regulation reference
SOX-404).

## Step 1: Submit Expense Report

The employee submits an expense report in the Concur system. Each line item must
include a receipt image, a business justification, and a project code. This step
is owned by the Employee role and requires the Concur system.

## Step 2: Automated Policy Check

An automated policy engine screens the report before manager review. Reports that
pass are routed automatically; exceptions are flagged. This step is owned by the
Compliance Bot role and requires the Concur system.

## Step 3: Manager Review

The direct manager reviews flagged expense reports. Expenses above $1000 now
require director sign-off. This step is owned by the Manager role and requires the
Concur system.

## Step 4: Finance Approval

The finance team performs the final approval and schedules reimbursement. This
step is owned by the Finance Analyst role and requires the SAP system. Approved
reimbursements are paid within 5 business days.
