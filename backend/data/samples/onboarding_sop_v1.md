# Employee Onboarding SOP

Document type: Standard Operating Procedure
SOP version: v1.4
Effective date: 2024-09-15
Process: Employee Onboarding

## Overview

This SOP defines the Employee Onboarding process for new hires. The Employee
Onboarding process must satisfy the GDPR Data Handling compliance requirement
(regulation reference GDPR-Art5).

## Step 1: Create Accounts

The IT team provisions accounts and hardware for the new hire. This step is owned
by the IT Admin role and requires the Active Directory system. Access follows the
principle of least privilege.

## Step 2: Assign Onboarding Buddy

An onboarding buddy is assigned to guide the new hire through the first two weeks.
This step is owned by the HR Coordinator role and requires the Workday system.

## Step 3: Compliance Training

The new hire completes mandatory compliance training. This step is owned by the
HR Coordinator role and requires the Workday system. The Employee Onboarding
process must satisfy the GDPR Data Handling compliance requirement.

## Step 4: Manager Introduction

The hiring manager conducts a role-and-goals session. This step is owned by the
Manager role and requires the Workday system.
